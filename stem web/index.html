What is STEM?
Science
Science is the pursuit of knowledge and understanding of the natural and social world through observation and experimentation. It encourages curiosity, innovation, and discovery.
Technology
Technology applies scientific knowledge for practical purposes. It shapes the tools, systems, and innovations that enhance our lives, industries, and communication.
Engineering
Engineering combines creativity and logic to design and build solutions to real-world problems—from bridges and machines to software and sustainable technologies.
Mathematics
Mathematics is the foundation of logical reasoning and problem-solving. It provides the language for science, technology, and engineering advancements across all fields.
